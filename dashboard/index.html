How the radar scores

Implied value = TVL × class multiple × growth factor × age factor. Every number comes from the sealed snapshot; no protocol name is ever special-cased.

FactorValueWhy
Vault curators× 0.22Risk curators and capital allocators earn fees on assets they manage, so they are priced like asset managers: a low multiple of managed capital.
Operating protocols× 0.55DEXs, lenders, yield and derivatives protocols capture protocol fees directly and their token has direct utility at launch.
Chains× 1.00A chain's token is the gas and governance asset of a whole network, so it carries the highest multiple.
Growth factor1 + clamp(7d %, −50, +100) / 100One week is noise. The clamp stops a single hot week from multiplying a protocol forty-fold, and a bad week from erasing it.
Age factor1 + 0.12 × min(years, 5)Longer without a token means more pent-up launch value, capped at five years so the oldest protocols cannot run away on seniority alone.

The board ranks every pre-token protocol the snapshot can honestly call a launch candidate: the 40 on the watchlist, plus every other protocol that records no token and is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades, and not an exchange. A protocol "records no token" when its DefiLlama record carries no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol. That is a record, not proof: a protocol on the list may still have a token its record does not carry.

The implied value is this radar's own claim, never a prediction that a token will be issued, or when, or at what price. Farm plans are generated from each protocol's own category, chains, TVL and age; they assume no points program exists unless the protocol's own app says so.
